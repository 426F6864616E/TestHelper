Get started in a minute

No credit card, no sign-up with us. Just a free key from any AI provider.

Install

Download TestHelper-1.0-Setup.exe and run it. The installer follows your Windows language.

Add a free key

Open AI providers and paste a key from Gemini, Groq or any other provider.

Scan

Click Launch browser, open your quiz and press Scan or F8.

Works with:

Google GeminiGroqOpenRouterMistralCerebrasAny OpenAI-compatible APIOllama / LM Studio (local)
Use it responsibly. TestHelper is meant as a study aid: to check yourself and learn from the explanation. Don't use it where the rules forbid it (exams, certifications). AI answers can be wrong.
